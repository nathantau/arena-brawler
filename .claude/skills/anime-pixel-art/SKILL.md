# Anime pixel art (showcase style)

Reference: Flint's bust, `assets/title/anime/flint_bust.png` (approved 2026-11-06), generated by
`tools/sprites/anime_busts.py`. Copy its structure for every new fighter so they all match.

## Pipeline (the only one that worked)

1. **Draw a smooth vector illustration in code** at 8x the target (96 px bust -> 768 x 1024
   source): PIL `ImageDraw` polygons, curves from `smooth()` (Catmull-Rom) and `centreline()`
   (flame tongues), ~7 px source outlines (`LINE`). Shapes are authored on a 48 x 64 **design
   grid** (`K` / `F` scale it), so proportions stay put when the output size changes.
2. **`pixelize(src, 96, 40)`** (`tools/sprites/pixelize.py`): line-art-aware box downscale;
   dark lines survive as crisp pixels instead of averaging into mush.
3. **`snap()`** every pixel to the fighter palette (gameplay colours + a few ramps), then
   **`cleanup()`** strays and **`recolor_interior()`**: near-black ink only on the outer
   silhouette, eye lids and mouth interior; interior lines become the area's dark tone (maroon on
   skin, dark navy on cloth, dark brown on leather).
4. Review sheets at x4 and x1 (`flint_bust_review.png` / `_clean.png` in the scratchpad), LOOK,
   iterate, then show the user the clean sheet.

What failed (don't repeat): placing pixels directly / ASCII heads / procedural primitives at
target size (faces "weird, not cute"), 4-5 soft bands, three near-identical variants, and
pixelizing someone else's character for shipped art (study only, never commit references).

## Style rules (96 x 128 bust)

- **Palette**: the fighter's gameplay colours (sample `assets/sprites/<id>.png`) + ~6 ramps
  (a light and a deep version of the main skin / hair, light + dark cloth, light + dark leather):
  ~15 colours total. Flat cel shapes, no dithering, no gradients.
- **Light**: key light top-left (lit tone on left planes), hard shadow shape on the right of the
  face / under cheekbone / under jaw and collar, plus a warm rim from the fighter's element on the
  far edge (Flint: light-orange rim on the right cheek and shoulder).
- **Face** (shonen male): face a little wider at the cheekbones, jaw angled into a **small
  slightly rounded chin** (not a point, not a diamond, not an egg); **ears** at eye-to-nose
  height (C shape, maroon inner curve), partly covered by side locks; nose = one shade stroke;
  calm mouth = short flat line; shout = clean dark open shape with a cream teeth row.
- **Eyes**: narrow and intense, outer corner slightly up, **straight heavy upper lid** (no lash
  flicks — they read as eyelashes), clean almond iris with a highlight, iris tall enough that it
  doesn't read sleepy; short V brows sitting just above the lids. One hairline — stacked
  hairline + bangs + brows read as goggles.
- **Elemental hair (fire)**: NO ink lines in or around the flames. Each tongue: deep-red edge,
  red tip, orange body, yellow core at the root; tongues separated by colour, not lines; wavy
  asymmetric edges with small licks; most lean one way like a flame in wind, a couple rise on the
  other side; **side locks** come down over the top half of the ears; a yellow / light-orange
  glow band at the hairline; rising 1-2 px sparks (yellow -> orange -> red), not leaf wisps.
- **Clothes**: collar with a cast shadow, folds as dark crease + lit lip, lit shoulder edge,
  props from the gameplay design (Flint: navy top, brown strap with stitching + buckle).
- **Frames**: calm sway A, calm sway B (flame tips / licks / bends change = flicker), shout,
  squint. Expressions swap eye / brow / mouth drawing functions on the same head.

## Adding a fighter

Build on Flint's functions in `anime_busts.py` (see the Sakura section below for how: `cols=`/`bands=`,
`snap(palette)`, a config dict, prefixed globals): reuse `FACE`/`NECK`/`TORSO` as the starting
proportions, swap the hair for the fighter's own (hair that is not elemental keeps a thin dark outline
but in a dark hair tone), sample the palette from the gameplay sheet, and write
`assets/title/anime/<id>_bust.png`. Female / cute faces: follow "Female characters" below.

## Female characters (Sakura, approved 2026-11-06)

Reference: `assets/title/anime/sakura_bust.png`, code = the Sakura section of `tools/sprites/anime_busts.py`
(`SCFG` / `SDEFAULT`, `s_*` functions, `FACE_OPTIONS`). She is Flint's pipeline with her palette mapped role for role
(skin ORANGE/LIGHT_OR/RED/DEEP_RED -> SK/SK_L/SK_S/SK_D, cloth NAVY -> KIM...), ~22 colours.

**Rules of the build**
- Derive from Flint's functions, never fork: `tongue(..., cols=(edge, colour_fn, shade, lit), bands=[...])` restyles hair
  (defaults keep Flint identical), `snap(art, mask, palette)`, `cleanup`, own `s_recolor_interior`. Look = one dict
  (`SCFG`: eyes, iris, hair, cheeks, shade); variants are dict edits, not copies.
- NEVER add a global that Flint uses (`H`, `W`, `CAP_TOP`, `BANGS`, `F`, `K`...): it silently changes Flint. Prefix hers
  (`S`, `SK_`, `HR`, `SBANG_SETS`) and run `cmp assets/title/anime/flint_bust.png <copy before>` on EVERY run.
- `pixelize` darkens line pixels ~0.6x before snapping: draw hair edges / brows / lips as `lc(colour)` (= colour / 0.6) so they snap
  to the intended tone, not INK. Thin lines (<8 source px) vanish: lashes >= 8, brows / small features go on AFTER pixelize.
- `rect()` / `ellipse()` ignore `YOFF` (the mouth offset -2.7): subtract it by hand when mixing with `poly()`.
- Draw order: back hair mass -> torso -> long locks / ponytail / buns / braid (BEHIND the face, so they never cross it) -> face
  -> cap, gloss band, bangs, side locks, clip. Hair: two passes (all outlines, then all fills) = line round the mass, none between locks.

**Face** (design units, 48 x 64 grid): `SFACE0` = Flint's polygon rounded: cheeks 35.6 at y 31.5, jaw soft, chin 8 wide at y 43,
`cheeks` 0.03 scales it. Flint's shading with one change: a jaw-following band (~3 px: `facem` minus itself shifted
(-1.5, -1.4) units, only below y 29) in SK_S, never a diagonal cheek plane. Keep ink outline, lit left, deep strip + rim right,
chin shadow on the neck. Neck short (`STOFF` 0.8 vs Flint's 3.5), shoulders softly sloped.

**Chin is separate from cheek width (2026-11-06, Shade feedback)**: "a tad pointier" / "revise how we draw females" means the CHIN TIP ONLY.
The first attempt tapered the whole lower face (narrower cheeks) and was rejected: "dont change cheek size". Rules: cheek width is its own
number (`cheek0`: Sakura +0.03, Shade -0.03, scales x of every point below y 27 as `s_face_pts` does) and the jaw corners / cheek points
(`SFACE0` down to (31.8, 39.4)) never move; the chin only replaces the last points of the polygon (below y ~40): chin width 8.0 and tip at
y 43.0 is Sakura's round chin, Shade ships **chin 6.5 wide, tip 0.8 below 43.0** (soft small point, never sharp). Steps for comparing
(`F_CHIN_STEPS` in `tools/sprites/anime_flicker.py`, `f_face_pts` patched over `ab.s_face_pts` at runtime, anime_busts.py untouched):
A (8.0, 0), B (7.2, 0.4), C (6.5, 0.8), D (5.4, 1.6). Young / cute = A, adult / cool = C-D. Keep the cheeks wide enough that the ears stay
hidden (narrowing the cheeks exposed dark ear notches). The jaw shadow band follows the polygon automatically. Render `female_chins.png`
(Shade + Sakura at the four steps) to compare before changing a face.

**Eyes** (option B = `EYES["animeB"]`, drawn by `s_eye_anime`): ~6.6 wide x 5.2 tall (never bigger; chibi / hearts / "alien"
came from big dark irises and a V lower lid), centres 24 +- 4.8, cy 27.8. Upper lash = thick ARCH peaking at t 0.62 (outward of
centre), 8 -> 12 source px, with a small wing at the outer corner; lower lid nearly flat, a thin light line on the OUTER third only.
Iris = TALL oval (irx 2.0, iry 2.55, ~60% of width, shifted 0.3 outward so the whites are mostly inner), top tucked under the lid;
bands: dark top (to cy - 0.2 iry), amber, light amber glow at the bottom; small dark pupil; big white highlight top-outer (r 1.0)
+ tiny one lower-inner. **Sample the eye boxes with `resize(NEAREST)` + snap, not the box-blended pixelize** (blending made brown
rings and stray colours). Iris colours via `IRISES` (mid, dark, light); amber / teal contrast with pink hair, rose does not.

**Brows**: `add_brows()` draws exact pixels after the pixel art: plum (`H_EDGE`), 1 px, 2 px at the inner end, high soft arch
(base cy - 3.9); determined tilts ~1 px down-in. Bangs are raised (tips y ~21.5 - 22.2) so brows sit on skin, not plum-on-pink.

**Nose / mouth / blush**: no drawn nose, a tiny `SK_S` tick (1.0 x 0.5 units) at (24.6, 33). Mouth (`s_mouth`, `LIP` tone via `lc`):
calm = curved smile 4.2 units wide, corners lifted, 1-2 px lighter lower-lip tint (`BLUSH`) under it; determined = same width,
right-lifted smirk; shout = rounded-D (flat top, round bottom, 3.5 x 2.3), dark plum inside, 1 px cream teeth, pink tongue.
Blush = flat pink ellipse under each eye (no dither). Too-small mouths were rejected: keep it >= 6 px wide at x1.

**Hair** (bob shipped): rounded bounce silhouette (`BACKS["bob"]` curves in under the jaw), 3 locks a side
(`locks()` + `LOCK_BANDS`: broad lit left band, shade right band, wavy edges, tips swaying 2-3 px), one dark back mass, gloss band
across the crown (H_L with an H_HI core), rounded bang tips, blossom `clip()`, 4 petals instead of sparks, tiny blossoms on the obi.
Other styles exist as `SCFG["hair"]`: long, pony, buns, halfup, braid.

**Workflow**: the user picks by eye, so render a variant sheet: 4 options on the SAME head (`face_variants_sheet`: x4 on top, x1
below, Flint calm at the left, labelled A-D) before refining; iterate in small steps and LOOK at x4, x1 and a x10 face zoom.
